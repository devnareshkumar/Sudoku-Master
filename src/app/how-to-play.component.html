How to Play Sudoku: Rules & Strategies

Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to sharpen your logic skills, this guide will walk you through the essential rules, basic techniques, and frequently asked questions about Sudoku Studio.

The Basic Rules

Sudoku is a logic-based number placement puzzle. The objective is to fill a 9×9 grid with digits so that each column, each row, and each of the nine 3×3 subgrids (also called "boxes" or "regions") contain all of the digits from 1 to 9.

  • Rule 1: Every row must contain the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.
  • Rule 2: Every column must contain the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.
  • Rule 3: Every 3×3 box must contain the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.

Winning Strategies

Scanning / Crosshatching

This is the easiest technique for beginners. Focus on a single number (e.g., 5) and scan all the rows and columns to see where a 5 is already placed. Use those existing 5s to eliminate empty squares in a specific 3x3 box until only one valid square remains.

Naked Singles

Sometimes, a specific cell can only possibly hold one number because the other eight numbers are already present in its sharing row, column, and 3x3 box. Whenever you spot a cell restricted to a single digit, fill it in immediately!

Using Note Mode (Pencil Marks)

As you progress to Medium and Hard difficulties in Sudoku Studio, keeping track of possibilities becomes essential. Tap the "Pencil" icon to toggle Note Mode. This allows you to write tiny candidate numbers inside empty cells. If you determine a cell can only be a 3 or an 8, mark both down. As you solve other parts of the grid, you will systematically eliminate these pencil marks until the solution reveals itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be good at math to play Sudoku?

Not at all! Despite using numbers, Sudoku requires absolutely no arithmetic or math skills. The numbers merely act as distinct symbols. You could replace the numbers 1-9 with letters, shapes, or colors, and the logic of the game would remain exactly the same.

Should I ever guess a number?

No, guessing is highly discouraged. Sudoku is a game of pure deduction. Every properly designed Sudoku puzzle has one unique solution that can be reached entirely through logical steps. If you guess wrong, you will eventually cause an unfixable error later in the game.

What happens if I make a mistake in Sudoku Studio?

Sudoku Studio tracks incorrect placements. If you place a number in a cell that violates the puzzle's final solution, it will be marked as an error in red. Accumulating 3 mistakes will result in a Game Over, encouraging careful, thoughtful play.
